Spread rating update

Runs as soon as a game is final and the closing spread is available. It predicts the team-level residual in the next game's closing spread after removing home-field advantage and the market's rolling six-week team ratings.

  • Final scoring margin
  • Closing spread and ATS margin
  • Closing spread magnitude
  • Close-game indicator
  • Play-by-play excluded after failing the 2025 incremental holdout

Verified target: next-game closing-spread market innovation after removing 2.5 points of home field and both teams' rolling six-week market ratings.

Shadow spread = 50% target-excluded market rating + 50% updated SP+ when independent market support exists; otherwise updated SP+ fallback. Before a team plays, updated SP+ equals its entering SP+ snapshot.

PROJECTED MARKET VALUE: Spreads use a validated two-tier actionable-versus-neutral display. Three colors were not forced because locked 2025 positive-CLV rates were not monotonic.

Total update

Runs after complete PBP is available and garbage-time filtering succeeds. It predicts the next game's closing-total innovation versus the rolling market-implied offense and defense baseline.

  • Pregame total and final-score surprise
  • Garbage-time-filtered PBP from both teams
  • Offensive and defensive performance signals
  • Reprojects the teams’ next-game total

Verified target: next-game closing-total innovation versus the rolling market-implied offense/defense baseline.

Shadow total = 60% predicted SP+ component total + 40% existing projected total - 1.1573.

PROJECTED MARKET VALUE: Total green/yellow/red tiers use 2024-selected thresholds and were monotonic on locked 2025 closing-line value.
