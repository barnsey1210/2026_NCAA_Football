# Returning Production Line-Movement Result

No returning-production feature validated as an early-season line-movement
signal.

- 2023 development: 78 qualifying FBS-vs-FBS games
- 2024 validation: 65 games
- Twelve frozen tail tests
- Validated signals: 0
- 2025 was not evaluated

The most directionally interesting spread result was a low home offense versus
away defense RP matchup: 2024 movement averaged 1.07 points toward the away team,
but development movement was only 0.21, validation direction accuracy was 46.7%,
and the adjusted q-value was 0.44. It is not a signal.

Returning production remains useful descriptive early-season context, but this
historical sample does not show that it reliably predicts where the opener will
move. No RP movement angle should be added to the 2026 site from this test.
